01 / BUSINESS QUESTION

Where and when is supply pressure repeating?

The analysis drills from city → zone → hour. It combines ride volume, missed-ride rate, assignment time and repeated problem days so the output ends with a priority list rather than a chart-only dashboard.

04 / DECISION LOGIC

What gets prioritised?

Recurring windows score higher when multiple signals worsen together:

  • Missed-ride percentage
  • Time to assignment
  • Historical ride volume
  • Number of days the problem repeats
The score ranks attention. It does not pretend to calculate an exact chauffeur headcount without separate availability data.

06 / OPERATING TAKEAWAY

What should teams do next?

Supply: review the highest recurring city + zone + time windows, strengthen or reposition coverage before those periods, then compare whether missed rides and assignment times improve on the next similar shift.

Operations: know the normal assignment range by city/zone, watch rides that remain unassigned beyond the usual threshold, and share repeated manual-intervention windows with Supply.

Weekly loop: review → prioritise → adjust → watch → compare again.
